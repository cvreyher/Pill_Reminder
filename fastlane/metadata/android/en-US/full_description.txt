MedicationTracker reliably reminds you to take your medication – or that of the people you care for. The app is in German.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Daily overview:</b> a clock with your progress, all doses in time order and a line for "now". Swipe to past days to see what was taken and what was missed.</li>
<li><b>Swipe to confirm:</b> tapping a dose opens a full-screen view with dosage, active ingredient and instructions; the intake is confirmed with a slider.</li>
<li><b>Reminders:</b> high-priority notifications on time, optionally audible even when the phone is muted, repeated until confirmed, and optionally shown like an alarm on the lock screen.</li>
<li><b>German medication plan (BMP):</b> scan the DataMatrix code of the "Bundeseinheitlicher Medikationsplan" and save it as a plan – with plan history, active/inactive plans and replacing plans with a summary of new, changed and discontinued medication.</li>
<li><b>For yourself or as a caregiver:</b> several people with their own plans, filters per person and automatic assignment of scanned plans.</li>
<li><b>History and calendar</b> of all intakes.</li>
<li><b>Local drug database:</b> import a CSV list of PZN and names so plans that only contain the PZN are named automatically.</li>
<li><b>Home screen widget</b> showing the next dose.</li>
<li><b>Material 3 Expressive</b> design with dynamic color and dark mode.</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
All data stays on the device. No account, no ads, no tracking. Only the optional lookup in the EMA drug database sends a medication name to the European Medicines Agency.

<b>Note</b>
The app does not replace medical or pharmaceutical advice.
